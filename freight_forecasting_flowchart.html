4 Maritime Risk & Demurrage
Port Congestion & Queue Queue Theory
  • M/M/c Berth Queue Model: Arrival rate λ vs service rate μ
  • Expected Waiting Days: W_q = f(AIS vessels waiting, berth availability)
  • Paradip / Haldia Average: 2.5 to 6.0 days waiting time during peak coal intake
Turnaround = W_queue + (Cargo_MT / Discharge_Rate)
Demurrage Exposure Engine Cost Risk
  • Laytime Allowed: Based on agreed Charter Party (CP) discharge rate
  • Demurrage Rate: $18,000 to $32,000 per day (vessel size dependent)
  • Despatch Bonus: Half demurrage earned if discharged early
Allowed Days = Cargo_MT / CP_Discharge_Rate
Overstay Days = max(0, Turnaround - Allowed Days)
Demurrage Exposure = Overstay Days × Demurrage_Rate

MILP Mathematical Formulation Solver Spec
  • Decision Variable: x_{v,r,t} ∈ {0, 1} (Vessel v, Route r, Charter Day t)
  • Timing Variable: y_fix_now ∈ {0, 1} vs y_hold ∈ {0, 1}
min ∑_{v,r,t} x_{v,r,t} × [ LandedCost(v,r,t) + λ × Risk(v,r,t) ]

Subject to:
• ∑_{v,r,t} x_{v,r,t} × Capacity_v ≥ Cargo_Required
• t + VoyageTime(v,r) ≤ Plant_Deadline (T_crit)
• t ∈ Laycan_Window
• Draft_v ≤ PortDraft_r + M × z_{lightering}
• ∑_{v,r,t} x_{v,r,t} = 1 (Single shipment assignment)
